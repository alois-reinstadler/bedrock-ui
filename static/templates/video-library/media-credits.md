# Frame film credits

These are actual open films and an official trailer. Playback streams from Blender and Wikimedia Commons; no film files are bundled. Playback requires an internet connection. Local JPEG artwork consists of frames extracted from the corresponding films, and the English Tears of Steel subtitle file remains local.

Directed by Ian Hubert. © Blender Foundation / mango.blender.org. Licensed **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0**.

- [Official film and license](https://mango.blender.org/about/)
- [Original 720p film download](https://download.blender.org/demo/movies/ToS/tears_of_steel_720p.mov)
- [CC BY 3.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
- [Original English subtitles](https://download.blender.org/demo/movies/ToS/subtitles/TOS-en.srt), converted from SRT to WebVTT without translation.

Local files: `tears-of-steel.jpg`, `tears-of-steel.vtt`. Original credit sequence preserved. The standalone soundtrack has a separate license; this distribution is of the film, not the standalone soundtrack.

- [Remote playback](https://download.blender.org/demo/movies/ToS/tears_of_steel_720p.mov?download=1)
